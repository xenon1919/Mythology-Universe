topic: birth of Ganesha
---
According to a widely told account in the Puranas, the goddess Parvati wished for a guardian of
her own who would answer only to her. While her husband Shiva was away, she created a boy from
the turmeric paste and clay she used for her bath, and breathed life into him, naming him Ganesha.
She set him to guard the entrance of her chamber while she bathed, instructing him to let no one
enter.

When Shiva returned and tried to enter, Ganesha, not knowing who he was and following his
mother's instruction strictly, refused to let him pass. Shiva, unaware that this was Parvati's
son, grew angry at being denied entry to his own home and, in the ensuing conflict, struck off
Ganesha's head.

When Parvati emerged and saw what had happened, she was overcome with grief and anger, and Shiva,
realizing his mistake, promised to bring the boy back to life. He sent his attendants to find the
head of the first creature they came across facing north, which turned out to be an elephant, and
placed the elephant's head onto Ganesha's body, restoring him to life. Shiva then declared Ganesha
his own son as well, and blessed him to be worshipped before all other gods at the start of any
undertaking.
