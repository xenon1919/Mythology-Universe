topic: rivalry between Karna and Arjuna
---
Karna and Arjuna were rivals from the time they were both young students, both trained partly by
the same teacher, Drona, and both considered among the finest archers of their generation. Their
rivalry was made sharper by the fact that neither knew, for most of the story, that Karna was
actually Kunti's eldest son and therefore Arjuna's older half-brother.

Karna's loyalty to Duryodhana, who had honored him with a kingdom when others mocked his birth,
placed him firmly on the Kaurava side against the Pandavas, including Arjuna. Before the
Kurukshetra war, Kunti approached Karna privately and revealed the truth of his birth, asking him
to join his brothers. Karna, out of gratitude and loyalty to Duryodhana, refused to switch sides,
but promised Kunti that he would not kill any of the Pandavas except Arjuna, sparing her from
losing more than one son regardless of which of the two of them survived their duel.

The rivalry between Karna and Arjuna came to a climax on the battlefield, where the two finally
fought each other directly. During the duel, Karna's chariot wheel became stuck in the ground,
and while he was attempting to free it, Arjuna, urged on by Krishna, killed him with an arrow. It
was only after Karna's death that the Pandavas fully learned of his true parentage, and
Yudhishthira in particular grieved deeply on learning that they had killed their own brother.
