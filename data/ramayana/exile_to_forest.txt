---
source: Ramayana
category: event
section: Ayodhya Kanda
characters: Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Kaikeyi, Dasharatha, Bharata
topic: Rama's exile
---
As King Dasharatha grew old, he decided to crown Rama as his successor, a decision welcomed by
the people of Ayodhya. However, Kaikeyi, Dasharatha's second queen, was reminded by her maid
Manthara of two boons Dasharatha had once promised to grant her for saving his life in battle.
Manthara persuaded Kaikeyi to use these boons to have her own son Bharata crowned king instead,
and to have Rama exiled to the forest for fourteen years.

Bound by his word, Dasharatha was forced to grant the boons, though it caused him great sorrow.
Rama accepted the decision without protest, valuing his father's honor and his own duty above his
personal claim to the throne. Sita insisted on accompanying Rama into exile rather than remain
behind in the palace, and Lakshmana likewise refused to leave his brother's side. The three of
them departed for the forest together.

Dasharatha died of grief shortly after Rama's departure. Bharata, who had been away at the time
of these events, returned to find his father dead and himself made king in his brother's place.
Bharata refused to accept the throne, and travelled to the forest to beg Rama to return. Rama
declined, saying he could not break his father's word, but gave Bharata his sandals to place on
the throne as a symbol that Bharata ruled only as Rama's regent until his exile ended.
