source: Ramayana
category: event
section: Yuddha Kanda
characters: Rama, Ravana, Lakshmana, Hanuman, Vibhishana, Kumbhakarna, Indrajit, Sita
topic: war in Lanka
---
After Hanuman located Sita, Rama's army of monkeys and bears, led by Sugriva, marched to the
shore facing Lanka. To cross the sea, they built a great bridge of floating stones, remembered as
the Rama Setu, with Hanuman and others carrying boulders inscribed with Rama's name so that they
would float.

Before the war began, Vibhishana, Ravana's own brother, tried to convince Ravana to return Sita
and avoid war, but Ravana refused and cast Vibhishana out. Vibhishana then joined Rama's side,
sharing knowledge of Lanka's defenses that would prove valuable in the battles ahead.

The war that followed was long and fierce. Ravana's brother Kumbhakarna, a giant who slept for
long stretches at a time, was woken to join the battle and was eventually killed by Rama.
Ravana's son Indrajit, a powerful warrior skilled in sorcery, wounded both Rama and Lakshmana
with magical weapons before being killed by Lakshmana. Finally, Rama and Ravana met in single
combat, and Rama killed Ravana using a powerful weapon, ending the war. Sita was freed, and
Vibhishana was crowned king of Lanka in Ravana's place. Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana then returned
to Ayodhya, where Rama's exile had by then come to an end.
