---
source: Ramayana
category: event
section: Aranya Kanda
characters: Rama, Sita, Ravana, Lakshmana, Maricha, Surpanakha
topic: Sita's abduction
---
While living in exile in the Dandaka forest, Rama and Lakshmana were approached by Surpanakha, a
rakshasi (demoness) and the sister of Ravana, king of Lanka, who wished to marry Rama. When Rama
refused and Lakshmana mocked her, Surpanakha attacked Sita in anger. Lakshmana intervened and
wounded her, and she fled to her brother Ravana seeking revenge.

Surpanakha told Ravana of Sita's beauty and urged him to abduct her. Ravana enlisted the demon
Maricha, who disguised himself as a golden deer and lured Rama away from their forest dwelling at
Sita's request, since she wished to have the beautiful creature. With Rama gone in pursuit and
Lakshmana drawn away by a cry for help (a trick, since the cry only imitated Rama's voice),
Sita was left alone. Ravana then approached disguised as an ascetic, and when Sita stepped past
the protective boundary Lakshmana had drawn, he seized her and carried her away to his kingdom
of Lanka in his flying chariot.

As Ravana carried Sita away, the aged vulture Jatayu attempted to stop him and fought Ravana in
the sky, but was mortally wounded. Before dying, Jatayu was able to tell Rama and Lakshmana what
had happened and in which direction Sita had been taken, giving them their first clue in the
search that followed.
