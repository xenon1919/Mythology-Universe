topic: search for Sita

In their search for Sita, Rama and Lakshmana allied with Sugriva, the exiled king of the monkey
kingdom of Kishkindha, agreeing to help him reclaim his throne from his brother Vali in exchange
for help finding Sita. Among Sugriva's followers was Hanuman, a monkey of immense strength,
devotion, and the son of the wind god Vayu, who became one of Rama's most loyal companions.

Hanuman was chosen to lead the search across the sea to Lanka, since he possessed the power to
grow to an enormous size and to leap great distances. He leapt across the ocean to Lanka, and
after searching the city, found Sita held captive in a grove called Ashoka Vatika, guarded by
demonesses. Hanuman revealed himself to her, gave her Rama's ring as proof that he came from
Rama, and offered to carry her back at once, but Sita refused, saying it was Rama's honor to
rescue her himself.

Before leaving, Hanuman allowed himself to be captured and brought before Ravana, and used the
opportunity to warn Ravana to return Sita or face war. When Ravana's men set fire to Hanuman's
tail as punishment, Hanuman used the burning tail to set fire to large parts of Lanka before
leaping back across the sea to report to Rama that Sita had been found.
